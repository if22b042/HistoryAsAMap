About

The idea

I wanted to display historical data in a different way. Instead of reading through long articles or scrolling through timelines, you can see history on a map. This lets you explore how events connect across geography and time, making history feel more tangible and easier to understand.

How it works

Events come from English Wikipedia articles. Anyone can suggest one: the site reads the article's date, location and introduction, the contributor checks them, and an editor reviews the event before it is published. The goal is a growing, reliable map of history that anyone can explore.
