Our Mission

History As A Map transforms how we explore and understand historical events by visualizing them across geography and time. We believe that seeing history spatially reveals patterns and connections that traditional formats often miss.

Data Source

All events are sourced from English Wikipedia articles, ensuring accuracy and reliability. Our community-driven approach allows anyone to contribute verified historical events to our growing database.

Technology

Built with Flask, Leaflet.js, and modern web technologies. Our platform combines interactive mapping with historical data to create an engaging educational experience.
