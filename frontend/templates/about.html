Learn more about our mission to map historical events across time and space

M

Our Mission

History As A Map is an interactive platform that visualizes historical events on a global map. We aim to make history more accessible and engaging by showing how events are connected across geography and time.

C

How It Works

Our system automatically extracts information from Wikipedia articles, including dates, locations, and descriptions. Users can contribute by submitting new events, which are then reviewed by administrators before being added to the map.

T

Technology

Built with modern web technologies, our platform uses Leaflet for interactive maps and Flask for the backend. We continuously improve our extraction algorithms to provide accurate and comprehensive historical data.
