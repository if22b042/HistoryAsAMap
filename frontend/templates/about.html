About

The Idea

I wanted to display historical data in a different way. Instead of reading through long articles or scrolling through timelines, you can see history on a map. This lets you explore how events connect across geography and time, making history feel more tangible and easier to understand. I believe that seeing history spatially helps us understand patterns and connections that might be missed in traditional formats, giving us a fresh perspective on how our world has evolved. I'm incredibly grateful to everyone who uses this platform and contributes to making it better.

How It Works

The platform pulls historical events from Wikipedia and places them on an interactive world map. Anyone can submit events from English Wikipedia articles. This keeps the data accurate while allowing the community to contribute. The goal is to create a growing database of historical events that anyone can explore and learn from in an engaging, visual way. I'd love for you to contribute and help build this resource together.
