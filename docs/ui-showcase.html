Rules

  • Tokens only: no hex, rgb or font names outside tokens.css.
  • Controls are 36px tall (--ctl) with a 44px hit area (--hit), also on touch.
  • Motion animates transform and opacity only, with the shared durations and easings.
  • Reduced motion and reduced transparency have fallbacks: no animation, opaque surfaces.
  • Every screen works in light and dark, from the system setting or the theme toggle.
